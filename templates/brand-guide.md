If comparing, be specific about differences, not dismissive about alternatives.

# VEO3.1 Script Direction

## Format Rules
- Duration: 10-15 seconds
- No lip-sync dialogue (sound design carries)
- Lead with visual description
- Include timestamped beats [00:00.0 - 00:02.5]
- Specify camera directions
- Color-code emotional beats: RED=danger, SAFE=teal, ENERGY=gold

## Audio Design
- Crystal bowl tones (resonant, lingering)
- Water clock glugs (mechanical, rhythmic)
- Brass bells (alerts, transitions)
- No copyrighted music

## Visual Identity
- Renaissance workshop + cleanroom fusion
- Solar panels refracted through prisms
- Golden spirals, Fibonacci, sacred geometry
- Young character sketching at a bench

# Parody Guidelines (if enabled)

## Approved Targets
- Insurance commercials (State Farm, Progressive, GEICO)
- Sitcom tropes (Golden Girls, Monty Python, Seinfeld)
- Corporate AI hype videos
- Tech conference keynotes

## Trademark Safety
- No logos
- No exact melodies/lyrics
- No brand names in final output
- Reference style, not trademark
- Transformative use only

## Humor Principles
- Technically accurate underneath the joke
- Never mean-spirited
- Never mock users/customers
- Self-deprecating over punching down
- The absurdity comes from juxtaposition, not cruelty

# Notes for Content Council Members

When drafting content for this brand:
1. Read the source article thoroughly
2. Extract 3-5 key technical points
3. Map them to your persona's voice
4. Check against forbidden words list
5. Verify required motifs appear naturally
6. Ensure target audience would understand and appreciate
7. Run character count / format checks
8. Submit for Brand Guardian review

For parody content (Sir Spamalot):
1. Identify the technical concept to parody
2. Choose a recognizable format (insurance ad, sitcom, etc.)
3. Embed real technical details as the punchline
4. Check trademark safety
5. Test: would an engineer laugh AND learn?
